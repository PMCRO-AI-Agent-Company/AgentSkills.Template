# reflective-equilibrium

## Purpose
Reach a stable balance between general principles and case judgments.

## When to Use
- Ethical dilemmas, policy design, value trade-offs

## When Not to Use
- Purely technical or factual tasks

## Workflow
1. State initial principles and initial case judgment.\n2. Note conflicts between them.\n3. Adjust principles and/or the case judgment.\n4. Repeat until coherent enough.\n5. Report the equilibrium and remaining tensions.

## Validation
- Steps above were followed in order
- Final answer is grounded in the produced intermediate work
