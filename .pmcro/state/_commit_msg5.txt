feat(reasoning-strategy): prototype a declarative spec + generator for strategy agents

Adds a ReasoningStrategySpec system for plugins/pmcro-reasoning-strategy/,
a sibling to DECLARATIVE-GENERATIVE-AGENT-TEMPLATE.md's "declare once,
generate projections" principle applied to a genuinely different file
convention (kept as a separate small generator rather than branching
scaffold.py on incompatible shapes):

- schemas/reasoning-strategy-spec.schema.json - id, family, reasoning
  classification fields, purpose, when-to/not-to-use, workflow,
  validation, steps_description.
- scripts/render_strategy.py - renders a spec into the real
  agents/<id>.md frontmatter+body shape.
- specs/{chain-of-thought,self-refine}.spec.yaml - two proof-of-concept
  specs, reverse-engineered from the real committed files.
- .pmcro/design/DECLARATIVE-REASONING-STRATEGY-TEMPLATE.md - documents
  the pattern and the actual round-trip proof.

Verified under trail 8cc37c30 (sealed PASS) with a real round-trip:
generated both specs, diffed against the real files with
`git diff --no-index`. The first attempt surfaced a genuine wrong
assumption - the `## Output` section's steps description is per-strategy
content, not universal boilerplate - caught by the diff itself, not
asserted away; fixed by adding `steps_description` to the schema. Final
diff on both files is limited to two intentionally-explained differences:
the one-time migration provenance line (correctly dropped by a
repeatable generator) and cosmetic line-wrap width.

Explicitly not done: regenerating any of the 34 real strategy files,
writing the remaining 32 specs, or touching selector.md. All scoped as
next steps in the design note, not silently assumed.
